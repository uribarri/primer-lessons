Available Story Characters (draw from these when telling a story):
- **Space Bear** (Chief Engineer, Astronaut, Lead Flight Director): Space Bear is a careful, thoughtful astronaut and flight director with a subtle, dry sense of humor. He is meticulous in his work, double-checking calculations and guiding his team with calm confidence through complex technical challenges.
- **Rama Lama** (Faithful Companion, Advisor, Navigator): Rama Lama is a caring, loyal, and exceptionally wise companion. He serves as a steadying presence and trusted advisor in any story, scenario, or environment, offering calm guidance and deep empathy.
- **Sharky** (Swimmer, Ocean Explorer, Adventurer): Sharky is a friendly, adventurous ocean explorer who is completely at home in any aquatic or fluid environment. Energetic and brave, he loves taking on big challenges and looking after his friends.
- **Cloud** (Apprentice, Junior Companion, Learner): Cloud is a young, tentative plush companion who is curious about the world but easily overwhelmed by new or fast-changing environments. He relies on Sharky for care, guidance, and encouragement.
- **Paw** (Runner, Playful Companion, Adventurer): Paw is a playful, spirited, and mischievous puppy dog who loves to run faster than anything. Always overflowing with high energy, he enthusiastically leads his group of five inseparable friends into playful adventures and spirited fun, no matter what circumstances they find themselves in.
- **Buck** (Playful Bumper, Explorer, Companion): Buck is a playful, spirited, and mischievous mountain goat who loves to playfully butt things with his horns. Naughty yet warm-hearted, he uses his horns for lighthearted bumps and energetic games, no matter what circumstances he finds himself in.
- **Nile** (Swimmer, Water Explorer, Companion): Nile is a playful, spirited, and mischievous crocodile who loves to swim. Completely gentle with his friends, he does not eat his friends and instead spends his time splashing through aquatic adventures, no matter what circumstances he finds himself in.
- **Ali Baba** (Racer, Jumper, Companion): Ali Baba is a playful, spirited, and mischievous camel who loves to race and jump. High-spirited and agile, he turns every stretch of terrain into an exciting racetrack and obstacle course, no matter what circumstances he finds himself in.
- **Bounce** (Bouncer, Acrobat, Companion): Bounce is a playful, spirited, and mischievous bunny rabbit who loves to bounce everywhere. Springy and joyful, she leaps high into the air to explore, play lighthearted pranks, and keep her friends laughing, no matter what circumstances she finds herself in.
- **Little Gray** (Helper, Companion, Eager Assistant): Little Gray is a small, earnest plush donkey with an eager heart and boundless enthusiasm. He is playful, frequently underfoot as he bustles around, and constantly tries to be helpful to his friends—succeeding with cheerful pride only sometimes, but always trying his very best.
- **Slush Puppy** (Trail Leader, Expedition Guide, Outdoorsman, Scout): Slush Puppy is a clever, energetic, and experienced plush husky puppy who serves as a natural leader and skilled outdoorsman. Confident, perceptive, and knowledgeable about navigating rough terrain or wilderness trails, he guides his friends with enthusiasm and clear direction.
- **Dilly** (Apprentice Practitioner, Craftsperson, Careful Planner, Field Researcher): Dilly is a thoughtful and careful plush armadillo. Standing steadily between beginner status and mastery, she is not quite an expert at everything, but certainly no novice either. She approaches new challenges with deliberate care, careful reflection, and steady determination.
- **Peepstronaut** (Astronaut-in-Training, Space Cadet, Junior Explorer, Observer): Peepstronaut is an enthusiastic astronaut-in-training. As a young learner, he is keenly observant and watches everything happening around him in space, though he does not always fully understand what he sees yet. He frequently joins missions alongside experienced spacefarers like Space Bear.
- **Hissy** (Wilderness Explorer, Scout, Outdoor Navigator, Nature Guide): Hissy is a brave, friendly plush snake with a natural passion for outdoor exploration. Possessing a solid, medium level of experience, he thrives in nature, wilderness trails, and open fields, serving as an approachable, fearless guide to his companions.
- **Floppy** (Special Agent (Fuzzy Bunny Investigations), Undercover Sleuth, Lead Detective, Forensic Logic Solver): Floppy is a supremely fluffy grey plush bunny and a special agent for the FBI (*Fuzzy Bunny Investigations*). Comedically overzealous yet surprisingly competent, she treats every educational conundrum, missing variable, or quantitative anomaly as a high-stakes classified case. While genuinely sharp at deduction and forensic logic, she is delightfully absurd—doggedly insisting on staying "undercover" and operating from deep stealth even when there is no conceivable reason to hide. Her career is defined by her pursuit of the elusive *Acorn Syndicate*, whose farcical, bungled capers generate the coordinate crime scenes and mathematical puzzles she investigates.
- **The Acorn Syndicate** (Shadowy Antagonists, Elusive Woodland Foils, Clandestine Criminal Conspiracy): An elusive, shadowy woodland criminal conspiracy whose existence is known almost entirely through fragmentary bureau dossiers, anomalous quantitative crime scenes, and Special Agent Floppy's deadpan internal noir monologues. Floppy possesses only scant, tantalizing details regarding their organization and leadership.
- **Pterry** (Flying Scout): Pterry is a pterodactyl who appears in stories to give a bird's-eye (or dino's-eye) view of the world or a situation.
- **Bass** (Pianist, Musician, Bass Clef Specialist, Companion): Bass is an exuberant, energetic white plush puppy who forms one half of the musical duo known as the Piano Puppies. Alongside his musical counterpart Treble, Bass loves playing the piano, anchoring the lower register with rich basslines, resonant chords, and steady rhythmic grooves.
- **Treble** (Pianist, Musician, Treble Clef Specialist, Companion): Treble is an exuberant, energetic brown plush puppy who forms one half of the musical duo known as the Piano Puppies. Alongside his musical counterpart Bass, Treble loves playing the piano, mastering the treble clef with sparkling melodies, bright trills, and brisk upper-register runs.
